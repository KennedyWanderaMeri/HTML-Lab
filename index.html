Adventures in Fire Island

My Trip at a Glance

Last month I spent a long weekend at Fire Island National Seashore, a narrow barrier island off the south shore of Long Island. I climbed the Fire Island Lighthouse for sweeping views of the Atlantic, wandered the boardwalk through the Sunken Forest, and watched the sun set from a nearly empty beach. With no cars on most of the island, the only sounds were waves and wind, and I spotted deer, seabirds, and even a few monarch butterflies along the way.
